START HERE

  1. Open Settings → Accounts. Add a Facebook account and finish signing in in the browser window.
  2. Choose that account in Active Account at the top. Check this before starting a task.
  3. Choose a task in the sidebar. Keep the app running while a task is working or waiting for its scheduled time.

ACCOUNTS: ADD, CHECK, OR SIGN IN AGAIN
  1. Open Settings → Accounts.
  2. Enter an account title and click Open Facebook Login.
  3. Sign in to Facebook in the window that opens. Complete any verification Facebook requests and wait for the app to save the login.
  4. Choose the account from the top bar when you want to use it.
  5. Use Check Login Cookies to check saved logins. If a login has expired, use Re-login and finish signing in again.

Delete removes an account from this app. It does not delete your Facebook account. Your portal login is separate from your Facebook login; Logout signs you out of the portal.

DASHBOARD: FIND A TASK QUICKLY

Open Dashboard in the sidebar for shortcuts to posting, listings, accounts, groups, and history. Click a task to open its page.

CREATE ONE LISTING MANUALLY
  1. Open Create Listings → Manual Posting.
  2. Choose the vehicle type and fill in the vehicle details, price, and description.
  3. Add photos (up to 20) and optional videos (up to 5). If a video upload fails or is unsupported, it is skipped and posting continues. Review the details before posting.
  4. Choose a delay if you want the post to run later.
  5. Choose the posting action. For group posting, select the groups. For posting to multiple accounts, select the accounts in the account window.
  6. Open Scheduled Posts to check the job.

Use Settings → Groups first if the group selection is empty.

CREATE SEVERAL LISTINGS FROM EXCEL
  1. Open Create Listings → Excel Posting.
  2. Choose your Excel file. Put each listing on its own row, with its vehicle details, price, description, and supported image fields.
  3. Choose the delay unit and value to set the time between posts.
  4. Submit the file and check any messages about missing details or images.
  5. Open Scheduled Posts to follow the queue.

Check the active account before uploading. Keep the app running until the queued work finishes.

MANAGE LISTINGS: IMPORT, REVIEW, AND DELETE
  1. Open Manage Listings and click Import Listings.
  2. Wait while the app saves listing details and photos. Use Stop Import if you need to stop.
  3. Search or select listings. Open a listing to review its details and saved photos.
  4. Click Refresh Clicks to update listing click counts from Facebook.
  5. To remove a listing, open it and click Delete listing. Choose Delete from Facebook, Delete from database, or Delete both.

Deleting from Facebook removes the live post. Deleting from the database removes the app's saved backup. Clear All Listings clears saved app listings; it does not delete Facebook posts. For bulk Facebook deletion, select listings and use Delete from Facebook.

REPOST A SAVED LISTING
  1. Import your listings in Manage Listings, then open the listing you want.
  2. Click Repost listing.
  3. Review or change the new listing's details. Arrange the photos; the first photo becomes the cover.
  4. Choose to repost now or schedule it for later, then confirm the action.
  5. Open Repost Status to see its schedule, progress, and result.

Reposting deletes the original Facebook listing first, then publishes the new one. Your saved backup is kept even if posting fails. Review the result before retrying. Clear history clears the repost history display.

CROSS POST TO OTHER FACEBOOK ACCOUNTS
  1. Open a saved listing in Manage Listings and click Post on Other accounts.
  2. Choose the other accounts that should receive the listing.
  3. Review the details, photo order, and timing, then confirm cross posting.
  4. Check Scheduled Posts for the source or destination account. Cross posts run one at a time.

Each destination account needs a working Facebook login.

IMPORT OR ADD FACEBOOK GROUPS
  1. Choose the Facebook account at the top, then open Settings → Groups.
  2. Click Import Buy and Sell Groups and wait for the list to load. You can stop an import using the stop control.
  3. To add a group individually, paste its Facebook group link into the add-group field and submit it.
  4. Search the saved groups and select entries to remove if needed.

Removing a saved group from this app does not make you leave the group on Facebook.

SCHEDULE A POST FOR SEVERAL GROUPS
  1. Open Settings → Group Posting.
  2. Write the description that you want to use for every group.
  3. Paste the group links, one link per line.
  4. Add photos if needed and choose the delay unit and value.
  5. Click Schedule Group Posting and follow the page's progress messages.

The account must be able to post in each group. A group's approval process can affect when a post becomes visible.

SCHEDULED POSTS AND REPOST STATUS

Open Scheduled Posts in the sidebar to see queued posts, posting times, and statuses for the selected account. The table refreshes automatically. Use its delete action to remove a queue entry, and review the displayed status first.

Open Repost Status for repost schedules and history. Keep the app running and your PC awake until jobs finish. In the installed desktop app, schedules are saved when you restart. Closing the window keeps the app running in its tray. If your PC is off or asleep, jobs wait until it is available again. Interrupted posts are marked for review so the app does not post them twice.

ACTIVITY LOG: CHECK WHAT HAPPENED

Open Settings → Activity Log to read recent automation messages. If a task fails, check its message, confirm the account is signed in, and fix any missing details before retrying. Clearing the log removes saved activity messages.

HEADER CONTROLS: ACCOUNT, BACKGROUND MODE, AND STORAGE
  • Active Account: choose which Facebook account you want to work with.
  • Background: Hidden runs supported automation without showing its Chrome window. Visible shows the browser. Login and verification may still need a visible window.
  • Temp / Uploads: show the space used by screenshots and manually uploaded images/videos.
  • Logout: sign out of the app's portal.
  • On a small screen, use the header arrow to collapse or expand the header.
CLEAN UP: FREE SPACE AFTER POSTING
  1. Wait until posting jobs have finished, including scheduled jobs that need uploaded photos.
  2. Open Settings and find Clean Up.
  3. Review the screenshot and upload sizes, then click Clean Up.

This deletes saved screenshots and manually uploaded images/videos. It keeps accounts, listing backups, and Facebook posts. Cleanup is disabled while listing posts are running or queued for any account.

BACKGROUND SYNC AND APP UPDATES
  1. Open Settings to control Background Sync and Start With Windows. Both are on by default in the installed app.
  2. The app imports each account's latest listings once every 24 hours and refreshes clicks every 4 hours. It waits for your current automation to finish.
  3. If the PC has been off, each overdue task runs once when the app resumes. Missed refreshes are not repeated.
  4. Close the window to keep the app in the tray. Right-click its tray icon and choose Exit App to stop it.
  5. When you are told an update is ready, open Settings, click Check for Updates, then Download & Install. Finish any running automation first.

Normal updates keep accounts, Facebook sessions, groups, and listings. An update that resets saved listings asks you before installing. Facebook may still occasionally require a fresh login.
